Where it comes from · feature C

C · From the tablet to real life

Linking the game to a real activity, without confusing what happens on the tablet with what happens at home. For each choice, the literature that supports it.

The trial with the real backpack

In the platform. After the game the adult can have the child pack the real backpack and note how it went: independently, with help, not achieved, not observed.

Why: what the literature says. In a randomized trial with 54 children under six, the FindMe app was liked and used, but did not change communication outside the game: using an app is not enough for a skill to transfer.

Fletcher-Watson et al., 2016

How far it goes. It concerns that app and that goal: it does not show that every game is useless.

Kept apart from the tablet data

In the platform. Game and real objects are read side by side and never added up; «not observed» is not a failure.

Why: what the literature says. In the 21 tablet studies reviewed, fewer than a third measure generalization and maintenance well. In 1977 the «train and hope» attitude was already criticized.

Hong et al., 2018 · Stokes and Baer, 1977

How far it goes. A correct answer on the tablet, even with a new photo, is still a trial on the tablet.

No promise of efficacy

In the platform. The game measures precise outcomes, one at a time; time spent in the app is never a measure of success.

Why: what the literature says. Of 14 randomized trials with digital technologies, 12 report improvements, but with very different technologies and measures; a meta-analysis of 21 trials finds effects with moderate to very low certainty.

Scarcella et al., 2023 · Liu et al., 2025

How far it goes. Counting positive studies says nothing about the value of a new game for young children.

The child can always stop

In the platform. Pause and exit are always available, with no penalties, timeouts or error sounds. Acceptability is gathered with child, family and practitioners.

Why: what the literature says. The perspective of autistic children is often overlooked in game design, and many studies stop at isolated guidelines.

Abd El-Sattar, Omar and Mohamady, 2024

How far it goes. These principles do not replace children's participation in the project.
