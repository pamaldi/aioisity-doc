Where it comes from · feature D

D · Explained proposals, reusable preferences

The system proposes and explains, the adult decides, and what the adult confirms holds for that child. For each choice, the literature that supports it.

The system proposes, the adult decides

In the platform. At the end of a session the system may propose a single change, with the data that justify it. It is applied only if the adult accepts.

Why: what the literature says. Adapting difficulty already exists: Pickstar adjusts it during play, others use fuzzy logic or generative AI. The difference we test is another: telling the gesture apart from the choice, and leaving the decision to the adult.

Shohieb, Doenyas and Elhady, 2022 · Paengkumhag et al., 2025 · Lyu et al., 2024

How far it goes. Pickstar shows more engagement, not more learning; the fuzzy-logic pilot has no comparison group.

One change at a time, always reversible

In the platform. The rule that prepares the proposals is written down and versioned. Each change opens a new phase and can be undone.

Why: what the literature says. Procedures must be described so they can be replicated, and changes must be attributable to what was done. Single-subject research requires repeated measures, phases and replications.

Baer, Wolf and Risley, 1968 · Horner et al., 2005

How far it goes. A single before/after comparison is descriptive: it does not show that the change produced the improvement.

Rules, materials and profile kept apart

In the platform. Game rules, the family's photos and voices and the preferences confirmed by the adult live in separate parts, and the system blocks inconsistent settings.

Why: what the literature says. Apps for autism offer little personalization, which limits their usefulness. Personalization, though critical, «has not been addressed»; software variability techniques are proposed.

Maseri et al., 2021 · Lopez-Herrejon, Herrera and Sevilla, 2018

How far it goes. Reviews from 2018 and 2021. Carrying preferences over to a second game is still to be done.
