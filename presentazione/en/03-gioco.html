The game: child, adult, system

A web app for tablets, installable and usable offline. A session has five trials and an explicit ending.

The child

  • sees the object in the list and hears the instruction, even in a family member's voice
  • answers by dragging, or with two taps
  • always has pause, help, repeat and exit
  • meets no timeouts and no error sounds
  • plays with photos of their own objects

The adult

  • prepares photos and voices in a few minutes
  • chooses instruction, gesture, required precision, number of alternatives
  • decides on the system's proposals
  • notes the trial with the real backpack
  • exports the data, without photos or voices

The system

  • records choice, gesture, prompts, photo used
  • after each session checks whether the gesture is an obstacle
  • balances the positions of the correct answer
  • computes results with their margin of uncertainty
  • blocks inconsistent settings
