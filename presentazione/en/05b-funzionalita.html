The platform's four features

Four features follow from the goal. On pages A–D, for each: what the platform does and which literature supports it.

FeatureThe problem it tacklesWhat it does in the game
A · Answer the same question in another wayA failed drag is mistaken for a wrong choice.Records choice and gesture separately; if the gesture fails, proposes two taps or a more tolerant backpack.
B · Understand what the child really knowsA result may depend on the place, on a particular picture or on a prompt, not on the object.Balances positions, uses several photos of the same object, records prompts, gives results with their margin of uncertainty.
C · Link the tablet to real lifeSuccess on the tablet is taken for success at home.A trial with the real backpack, noted by the adult and kept apart from the game data.
D · Explained proposals, reusable preferencesThe game changes on its own without saying why, and every game starts from scratch.Proposes one change at a time with the data, the adult decides; what the adult confirms will also serve in the next game.

Each feature leads to its own page, with the literature that supports it.

Why the backpack: a simple matching task, tied to a real routine in the child's day, that can also be checked with the real objects.
