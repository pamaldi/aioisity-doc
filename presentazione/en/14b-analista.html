Closing

What we ask the analyst

The missing parts depend on their opinion. Here is on what.

  • Define the target skill and the mastery criterion.
  • Specify the prompt hierarchy, what counts as an independent response and how to fade prompts.
  • Say whether the «picks well but cannot drag» case occurs among the children they follow, and how often.
  • Tune the thresholds and compare, over a few sessions, the app data with direct observation.
  • Assess acceptability: does the child choose to play it? Do the family and practitioners find it useful?

The most important question: does the «picks well but cannot drag» case occur among the children they follow? If not, the idea must be rethought.
