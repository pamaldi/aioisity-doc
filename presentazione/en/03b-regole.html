The game

Five rules the game always follows

They hold in every phase of the project, including the next ones.

The question does not change

Trials are built without knowing which gesture will be used: changing the gesture cannot touch objects, alternatives or the correct answer.

The adult decides

The system proposes and explains; nothing is applied on its own, and every change can be undone.

Separate sources

What the adult confirms, what they observe with the real objects and what the system estimates stay apart: a system estimate never becomes an adult's choice.

Data stay on the device

Photos, voices and trials stay on the tablet; they are exported only by choice, without photos or voices.

No artificial intelligence in the game: if it ever comes, it will help the adult prepare materials, never the child during play.
