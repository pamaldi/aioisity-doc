Prototype · presentation route

Pack the backpack

The first game of a tablet platform designed for autistic children aged 3–8 (Italian title: «Prepariamo lo zaino»). It is a prototype: it has not yet been tried with children.

What is new

A missed gesture is not a wrong choice.

When the child gets it wrong, the game tells whether they chose the wrong object or chose well but could not complete the gesture. In the second case it proposes to the adult an easier way to answer, without changing the question.

Platform requirements

  1. Accessible to every childEvery gesture has an alternative; pause, help and exit always available; no timeouts or error sounds.
  2. The question does not changeAdapting the way of answering never touches objects, alternatives or the right answer.
  3. The adult decidesThe system proposes with the data; nothing applies on its own and every change can be undone.
  4. Ready in minutesHousehold photos and voices prepared by the adult; inconsistent settings are blocked.
  5. Reliable, separate dataChoice, gesture and prompts recorded apart; game, real objects and estimates are never added up.
  6. PrivateWorks offline, data stay on the tablet, no artificial intelligence in the game.
