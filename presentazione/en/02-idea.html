The game

Change the way of answering, not the question

The game records separately what the child chooses and how the gesture goes. If the choice is right but the gesture fails, it proposes an easier way to answer.

  1. 1 · Record separatelywhich object was chosen, how the gesture went, which prompts were present
  2. 2 · Wait for dataevaluates at the end of the session, over several trials and sessions, never during play
  3. 3 · Propose a changeonly one, explained with the numbers that justify it
  4. 4 · The adult decidesaccept, refuse or lock; every change can be undone

If the release lands near the backpack

It proposes a more tolerant backpack: the area that accepts the release grows, but the backpack the child sees stays the same.

If the drag is interrupted

It proposes two taps: touch the object, then the backpack. No dragging.

List, objects, positions and correct answer stay identical. Only the gesture the child answers with changes.

And if the choice is often wrong? Then the system does not propose changing the gesture: that is not where the problem is. The part of the system that reads the data after each session and prepares proposals is what we call the «engine».
