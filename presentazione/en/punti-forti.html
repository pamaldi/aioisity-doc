Prototype · presentation route

Strong points

What makes this platform different, point by point, and where to see it in the simulation and in the recordings of the real game.

Choice and gesture recorded separately

For each trial the game saves which object the child chose and, separately, how the gesture went: succeeded, released nearby, interrupted. A correct notebook left outside the backpack does not become a discrimination error, and the programme does not step back by mistake.

See: Feature 1 · Case A

Easier, with the same task

If the child chooses well but dragging fails, the game moves to two taps (first the object, then the backpack) or to a backpack that accepts releases a little farther away, looking the same. List, objects and right answer stay identical: an improvement reads as an improvement of the gesture.

See: Feature 3 · Case B

The system proposes, the adult decides

At the end of a session, never during play, the system may propose a single change, with the numbers that justify it («right choice 9 times out of 10, drag failed 6 times»). The adult accepts, refuses or locks; every change can be undone, and a refused proposal does not come straight back.

See: Feature 2 · Case F

Data you can trust

The right answer takes each place the same number of times, and the same object appears in different photos: this shows whether the child recognizes the object, not a place or a picture. Prompts are recorded, and with few trials the system uses a cautious estimate, not the raw percentage.

See: Feature 4 · Case I

From the tablet to the real backpack

After the game the adult can have the child pack the real backpack and note how it went: independently, with help, not achieved, not observed. The note sits next to the tablet data, never added: success on screen is never passed off as success at home.

See: Feature 5 · Case H

The child's own world, safely

The adult photographs the child's real objects and a family member records the instruction: in a few minutes the game uses the things and the voice of home. Inconsistent settings are blocked before they reach the child, and photos, voices and data stay on the tablet.
